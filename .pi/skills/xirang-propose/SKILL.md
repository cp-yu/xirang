Propose a new change or update an existing change by organizing the actual draft with the user. Reuse the confirmed design, discuss ideas and gaps discovered while writing, and complete the Semantic Delta and Change Plan for implementation. Explore maintains the intermediate design and feasibility evidence; Propose writes and reviews the complete Change without inventing material decisions.

## Readiness boundary

Before creating or updating Change artifacts, apply Documentation and gap handling to identify decisions requiring user judgment. Complete the necessary read-only discovery and permitted record maintenance, then stop for any unresolved material decision requiring user judgment that affects this target.

Before doing stage work, load the xirang-common skill from this tool's discovered skills or from ../xirang-common/SKILL.md relative to this skill file, unless its required guidance is already available and current in this Agent's project context. Keep the selected stage while loading it; do not route back or load this stage again. Follow common's on-demand guidance and reuse rules.

For a Kind or Contract with `contractFormat: business-specification`, read project-root file `.xirang/references/xirang-business-specification-standard.md` before exploring or authoring it, unless already available and current. Generic framework or organizational Elements do not trigger this format.

When designing verification, changing tests or reviewing test evidence, read project-root file `.xirang/references/xirang-test-quality.md` unless already available and current.

Before navigating or interpreting the Semantic Model, read project-root file `.xirang/references/xirang-model-context.md` unless already available and current.

Before exploring or writing an Element Declaration or Contract, read project-root file `.xirang/references/xirang-semantic-authoring.md` unless already available and current.

Before forming or reorganizing a hierarchy, run `xirang config project --select decomposition --json`, read the normalized `decomposition` path from its output, and read that complete guidance file. Stop structural work if it is unavailable; do not substitute another method. Contract-only work does not trigger this step.

## Confirmed design inputs

Load `.xirang/references/xirang-design-record.md` at entry unless already available and current. It owns record discovery, confirmation authority, reload/selective reads, partial CLI updates, user-facing presentation, writing rules, coverage and consumption. Apply its **User-facing record presentation** rules at record loads and after updates; apply other gates at their flow points below. Do not duplicate full reads per section. A record is formation evidence, not a complete Delta.

## Documentation and gap handling

- **Evidence and judgment**: Reuse confirmed decisions and still-applicable evidence. Before recommending an approach or treating it as ready, independently assess whether it is feasible and can achieve the intended outcome within the constraints. Obtain missing evidence and check its reliability and applicability; state your assessment and key reasons.
- **Local detail and faithful writing**: independently resolve local model organization from confirmed intent and modeling rules, handle minor details within the agreed scope and approach, organize cases, and correct expression or derivation errors; preserve the user's clear wording when it remains appropriate in the target model. Before presenting a draft and after a local revision, compare authored branch outcomes, defaults, error results, and restrictions with the confirmed source: check the triggering condition, action, affected operation or state, time boundary, and result. If the wording requires or forbids an outcome the source leaves open, identify that difference instead of treating it as a paraphrase. Remove unintended strengthening; use Decision gap when completing the target requires a substantive choice. A ruling limited to one local edit does not confirm other draft additions. Disclose permitted minor additions with their basis and locations at completion. Do not ask the user to fix an Agent writing error when the confirmed design already determines the answer.
- **Decision gap**: when completion would change a confirmed commitment, expand scope, or settle a material business or architecture trade-off, explain the gap and alternatives, recommend with reasons, then ask one focused question and wait before writing the choice as settled. Discuss and confirm small content or approach adjustments within Propose and update the shared record under its permission rules; a design discussion or record revision alone does not require changing workflow. Return to Explore when feasibility needs experimental judgment, the direction must be reconsidered, or substantial design must be redone; resume Propose after that work. A direct-Propose request, a request not to ask questions, or a readiness override does not supply a missing decision; report the blocker instead of guessing.
- **Propagation and disclosure**: after confirmation, record the decision through the shared protocol and reconcile the affected Delta and Plan together. Do not hide new observable behavior solely in design or tasks. Reconcile permitted minor observable details into the Contract and Plan and disclose their basis and locations, without labeling them as user confirmations; if retained for later discussion, they remain pending. Track additions in the conversation for the final report; do not create a supplement ledger or add authorship/confirmation status to normative Contract text. Completion disclosure does not replace prior confirmation for material decisions.

## Drafting and user review

For a simple change, write the complete draft, then present its concrete differences and remaining questions together for user review. For a complex change, organize drafting and review around one mechanism or a group of related Elements; show the actual draft for that group, including structural changes, reasons, and semantic impact, then continue from feedback. Do not mechanically ask for approval of each Element or treat the number of Elements as the review plan.

Preserve artifact dependency order while using these review units. Discuss new ideas and questions as they arise; do not optimize for completing one invocation without interaction. After a local revision, recheck the affected mechanism and artifacts. Unresolved decisions affecting the target block completion; artifact completeness and CLI success do not establish semantic readiness.

## Flow

1. Resolve a provisional kebab-case change ID. Ask one focused question when the requested change itself is unclear. Report status only at readiness, blocker, and final-summary points; do not emit per-artifact progress updates. Necessary design discussion and draft review follow the complexity guidance above.
2. Gather read-only evidence before any write.
   - Run `xirang list --json` and inspect relevant existing change artifacts when present.
   - Run `xirang record list --json`. Select a relevant Change-context record and apply the shared **Inputs and authority** protocol to the complete latest record, Summary, and current context. Unresolved conflicts and pending decisions affecting the target block Formation. With no record, check for an explicit user exemption or a statement that this design has no normative content; obtain an undeclared record decision here before compiling. Do not infer exemption or create an empty record.
   - Load the formal Xirang Semantic Model through the shared context above.
   - Follow shared Semantic Model context: search finite candidates, use outline and impact for navigation, then query selected Declarations and needed Contracts. Do not expand every impact result. Recover global understanding with markdown outline and selected reads; use snapshot only when those are insufficient or model diagnostics require it.
   - Use implementation evidence only where needed to resolve current behavior or lowering constraints.
3. Assess semantic readiness.
   - Reuse a confirmed, still-current `Design Summary` when the conversation contains one, and state that it is being reused. Route architecture decisions to proposal Architecture Source, `design.md`, and the Declaration, Relationship, Metamodel, and View Delta units; route testing strategy to `design.md` and goal-level verification directions to `tasks.md`; Apply chooses concrete test work; route risk and trade-off decisions to `design.md`.
   - Otherwise establish the problem, intended outcome, constraints, impact scope, approach and verification method through project evidence and focused questions, without treating a supplied approach as complete intent. Resolve material gaps, including Semantic Delta decisions across Definition, Contract and structural scope. Multi-subsystem scope is evidence, not an automatic Explore requirement; report a gap only when it cannot form one coherent change scope.
   - If readiness is incomplete, list the concrete missing items and use Documentation and gap handling: confirm an isolated decision here or recommend `/skill:xirang-explore` for substantial design work. Apply the Readiness boundary before any Change artifact write; CLI-managed record maintenance follows the shared confirmation rules.
   - If the user explicitly overrides the readiness recommendation, continue within that authorization, but the override does not authorize guessing source decisions. Ask one focused question at a time for every unresolved material behavior or architecture decision before authoring dependent content.
   - For an existing change, assess readiness from existing artifacts, current input, the confirmed Design Summary, formal source, and implementation evidence together.
   - Keep readiness, missing-item, and override state in the conversation only; do not copy it into change artifacts.
4. Resolve change identity after readiness passes or is explicitly overridden.
   - If the user explicitly requests a new change and the ID is unused, run `xirang new change "<name>"`.
   - If the user explicitly requests a new change and the ID already exists, stop and ask for a different ID. Do not overwrite, continue, or synthesize an alternative ID.
   - If the user explicitly requests an existing change, update that change in place without asking for another ID.
   - If intent is ambiguous and the ID exists, ask whether to update the existing change or create an independent new change; in non-interactive mode, fail and request an explicit choice.
   - Run `xirang status --change "<name>" --json` for `applyRequires`, artifact order, dependencies, and schema.
5. Determine source impact before writing `proposal.md`.
   - Determine Contract impact for the whole Change from the confirmed commitments and formal model. Identify the existing Elements responsible for each affected behavior at their respective abstraction levels, including parents carrying that commitment, then read their current Contracts before deciding which change. Reuse their identities. For each parent carrying an affected commitment, determine its target obligations from the complete confirmed design, including the Design Summary even when the partial record has no separate entry for that parent; do not narrow those obligations to what its current Contract already says. Compare them with the parent's current clauses alone: identify the clause that determines each promised outcome with its required conditions and limits. Child refinement does not remove a commitment assigned to the parent; a generic delegation or a clause merely consistent with the child rules does not establish that the parent states those obligations. If those clauses still permit a ruled-out outcome or leave a confirmed outcome undetermined, include the missing or changed commitment in proposal Behavior Source and the corresponding Section Entries. Preserve the conditions, numbers, exceptions, and timing that determine the parent's promised outcomes; child implementation detail need not be repeated. A no-op needs existing clauses that already carry those commitments, even when the Declaration is unchanged. Review groups control drafting order, not the whole Change's source scope. An optional-contract Element without a Contract does not by itself require a new one.
   - Compare structural impact with the formal Xirang Semantic Model. Identify affected Element Declarations, refinement, Relationships, Element Kinds, Relationship Kinds, and Authored Views. For every added or modified Declaration, write the complete target Definition, not a summary of what changed. Implementation movement or call/import evidence alone is not a structural change.
   - Preserve confirmed structural decisions. A partial record leaves room for unconfirmed structure: when source impact requires a new or reorganized hierarchy, apply the Structural Decomposition Guidance to the unresolved scope; form and check a complete sibling set breadth-first, then include proposed structure in the relevant draft review. Seek user confirmation before writing a target that depends on an unresolved material choice; do not require approval of each sibling set solely because it is being formed.
   - Compile the record through its shared **Writing rules**, including confirmed structure and concrete examples. Missing target behavior belongs in the host Contract, not Tasks. Resolve target-affecting pending decisions before dependent writing.
   - When Architecture Source is `None`, do not invoke decomposition guidance or invent hierarchy changes.
   - Determine the Contract and structural scopes of one Semantic Delta. Keep the compatible `Behavior Source` and `Architecture Source` proposal headings: `Behavior Source` lists `New Specs` or `Modified Specs` as the Element identities whose Element Contract is added or modified, and `Architecture Source` lists the identities whose Declaration, Relationship, Metamodel, or View semantics change. Both sections address the same identity space; they separate Contract impact from structural impact, not two kinds of identifier. Use `None` only when that scope truly does not change.
6. Generate ready artifacts in dependency order, using the drafting and review units above. For each artifact, read only `.xirang/references/xirang-artifact-<artifact-id>.md`: Definition first, then Guidance and Template. Query `xirang config project --artifact <artifact-id> --select proseLanguage --select context --select rules --json` for current authoring configuration. Reuse loaded static guidance within the conversation.
   - Follow the reference Guidance; use status for dependency and existing output paths. Keep static Definition/Guidance, current configuration and target content separate; do not copy guidance or configuration into artifacts.
   - For `proposal.md`, write `## Source Impact` with the compatible Behavior Source and Architecture Source sections, referencing Elements by `identity`.
   - When creating `specs`, write the Element Contract delta into `.xirang/changes/<name>/elements/<identity>.md` for exactly the identities declared under proposal `Behavior Source`; the frontmatter locates the host Element and the body carries the Section Entries. Read the exact Section titles from the formal Element Contract before authoring ADDED, MODIFIED, or REMOVED deltas. Express a rename as REMOVED old Section plus ADDED new complete Section. Author only canonical `### Section: <title>` headings; a Section body is free-form. Rely on combined change validation for deterministic header compatibility. Follow the returned Specs authoring contract.
   - Route obsolete-test rationale from **Test Maintenance** to `design.md` and goal-level behavior/verification directions to `tasks.md`; Apply decides concrete test updates/removals. Route **One-time Verification** items to goal verification directions as evidence-only checks with no persistent test file; absence assertions reference the removed Section.
7. Continue until all `applyRequires` artifacts are done, applying Documentation and gap handling to newly discovered omissions. Resolve local concept boundaries and hierarchy from confirmed intent and modeling rules; multiple compliant organizations alone are not blockers. When a remaining choice would change Element identity, scope, confirmed commitments, or a substantive trade-off, stop and ask one focused question before dependent writing. Otherwise choose a supported organization and present structural changes, reasons, and semantic impact in draft review. Handle only minor details autonomously and discuss substantive additions before settling them. Complete the applicable whole-draft or mechanism-group review before the closing gate.
8. After Specs and Design are complete, reconcile structural scope before writing the remaining Delta units.
   - Re-read proposal Architecture Source, `design.md`, the formal Xirang Semantic Model, and current implementation evidence.
   - If a user-confirmed decision recorded in Design changes structural impact across Declarations, refinement, Relationships, Kinds, or Views, update only proposal `Architecture Source` to declare final structural scope, then reconcile the affected Delta and Plan. Writing a choice in Design does not itself confirm it.
   - Write each affected Entry into its partition under `.xirang/changes/<name>/`: `elements/<identity>.md` frontmatter for a Declaration Entry, `relationships/<relationship kind identity>.yaml` for `{operation, source, kind, target}` entries, `metamodel/<kind identity>.md` for Kind Entries, and `views/<view identity>.md` for Authored View Entries. Every Entry carries `operation` and its complete target state; `REMOVED` carries identity only; `relationships/` has no `MODIFIED`.
   - If Architecture Source is `None`, omit structural Entries (Element Declarations, Relationships, Kinds, and Authored Views), not the `elements/` partition: preserve its host-locating frontmatter and all Section Entries required by Behavior Source; do not invent structural changes from Contract changes alone.
   - Validate the Expected Semantic Model with `xirang arch validate --change "<name>" --json` and fix all errors before continuing.
9. Check compilation scaffolding before semantic-source validation.
   - Reuse the proposal and design references already loaded; compare each file with its Definition and Template.
   - Read `.xirang/references/xirang-artifact-tasks.md` when authoring tasks. Author `## Goals` as the sole task format: each goal has a stable `G<n>` checkbox, a Contract Section reference, and a concise verification direction covering relevant behavior, boundary, and observable result. Do not include implementation files, test actions, test layers, commands, or nested Checks; Apply chooses these from current code and test evidence. Include only user-confirmed target ordering; ordinary technical dependencies belong to Apply.
   - Make verification directions concrete through Contract references. For each target behavior needing implementation, state the behavior, relevant boundary, and expected observable result without fixing a specific test, command, file, or test decomposition. Keep implementation and testing arrangements in Apply rather than duplicating them in tasks.
   - Cross-check goal boundaries and verification directions against complete Contracts and user-confirmed scope. Merge implementation surfaces that jointly deliver one target; do not split by component, module, directory, Section, or Acceptance Case count. Encode ordering only when explicitly confirmed by the user; leave ordinary technical sequencing to Apply.
   - Validate that tasks use only the goal-level format; old layouts are unsupported and are not migrated.
10. Run the closing consistency gate: first the plan consistency review, then the deterministic validation.
    - Review source to target first. Read the complete latest record, confirmed Design Summary and Propose rulings. For each applicable confirmed commitment, identify its responsible Elements at each abstraction level before consulting the authored scope. Read their actual target clauses, including unchanged clauses claimed as no-op; compare conditions, numbers, examples, timing, exceptions and outcomes separately at each owner. Apply the shared nature/decision/applicability rules: accepted applicable normative details need current locations and excerpts; resolve target-affecting pending decisions and justify non-applicability or no effect. Excluded and informative entries do not require invented obligations. Repair missing commitments in Contracts and proposal scope before reconciling Plan.
    - Then read the current proposal, Delta, design and tasks back to their authority. Check each behavioral requirement and asserted outcome against its Contract and confirmed basis, applying Documentation and gap handling to permitted minor elaborations and unresolved intent. A valid reference or matching topic does not establish agreement; compare what each statement requires, forbids or leaves open. Check every target-state occurrence of revised decisions, preserving accurate historical descriptions and unrelated text. Keep implementation and testing arrangements in Plan. Every target behavior needing implementation must have an implementation arrangement and an observable verification direction that can falsify it; no verification direction invents target behavior. Correct source-determined writing errors yourself; when a substantive decision is needed, present all findings and request one user ruling at a time. Agreement among artifacts cannot excuse a shared omission.
    - Challenge the complete mechanism and its acceptance outcomes: try relevant boundary or competing-event sequences, tracing initial state, event order and expected outcome against invariants, timing and failure behavior. Apply Evidence and judgment to the assumptions supporting those outcomes. An approach failure alone does not establish an intent mismatch or an unachievable goal. Do not substitute a list of scenario names for the reasoning. Repair expression or derivation errors without changing confirmed decisions; otherwise use Documentation and gap handling. A passing reference check cannot establish that a mechanism is coherent.
    - Then run the deterministic validation on the final artifacts after user rulings: `xirang validate --change "<name>" --preview --json`, then inspect the returned effective-diff entries against the intended scope before completion. This command performs combined validation and task structure validation. Do NOT run `xirang sync`.
    - Errors from deterministic validation or the scaffolding checks block ready-for-apply. Fix at most one round and recheck once; remaining errors stay blocking. Formatting repairs may only restate existing commitments; they must not invent restrictions to satisfy a validator. Recheck any affected semantic conclusions after repairs.
    - WARNING does not block ready-for-apply; retain it for the final summary.
11. Finish the reviewed Change under the shared **Coverage gate** and **Output and handoff** after combined validation succeeds.
    - Bind the latest fully reviewed record version and Expected Semantic Model. Reuse the closing gate's complete read while it remains current; changed source or relevant drift requires reload and renewed semantic checks.
    - Submit per-entry classified coverage and atomic details with current target excerpts via `xirang record coverage <filename> --change "<name>" --from <file> --json`. For Change targets this also checks the record's structure against the target. Source or target changes invalidate coverage; resolve rejection, recheck and revalidate before resubmission.
    - Keep the record active during user review. Present the validated Change and coverage result and wait for feedback. When the user requests changes, reconcile the Delta, Plan and any newly confirmed record content; revalidate and repeat coverage before asking for acceptance again. A passing CLI check is not user acceptance.
    - Wait for the user to accept the final Change and its current coverage result. Only then run `xirang record consume <filename> --change "<name>" --json`. It rechecks coverage freshness and structure without rerunning coverage. Verify target snapshots and source cleanup as the shared protocol requires. Any failure blocks Formation. The snapshots are formation history, excluded from later semantic parsing, Sync and Closure.
    - With no record, skip coverage/consume only under the declared record decision checked at entry; still wait for user acceptance of the final Change before declaring Formation complete.
12. Finish with `xirang status --change "<name>"`. Summarize artifacts created or updated and validation errors and warnings. In the conversation, distinguish reused Explore confirmations (or confirmed current input), minor details added autonomously during Propose, with their basis and artifact locations, new decisions confirmed by the user during Propose, and unresolved items and their effect on readiness. State explicitly when no supplements or new decisions were needed. Artifact completion and CLI success do not establish semantic readiness: unresolved material decisions affecting target behavior, scope or implementation approach block ready-for-apply; disclosed inferred details do not block readiness solely because they lacked separate confirmation. Report readiness for `/skill:xirang-apply-change` separately, without claiming unconfirmed content came from Explore. Do NOT generate a presentation artifact or run a separate Diff command.
    Separate observed CLI results, Agent semantic findings and remaining evidence gaps. Support readiness with concise findings from the actual semantic and dependency checks, identifying relevant artifact locations and any unresolved issue; do not infer coverage or coherence from artifact counts, valid references or declared dependencies. No full review transcript or new report artifact is required.

## Artifact Contract

Before authoring, read `.xirang/references/xirang-configuration-use.md` unless already available and current; follow its effective configuration and document-language rules.

Tasks use a single goal-level checklist. Each goal states the intended outcome, references its target Contract Section, and identifies behavior, relevant boundary, and observable verification direction. Apply determines files, tests, commands, and technical ordering from repository evidence. Only explicit user-required ordering is encoded in goal order.
